Privacy Policy — Abhyas AI: BPSC Prep

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to Abhyas AI: BPSC Prep for Android (package name: com.bpsc.guide), published by SOS Study (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The app is an independent educational product and is not affiliated with the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) or any government entity.

Information stored by the app

The app stores practice sets, saved questions, answers, scores, revision notes, daily news unlocks, interview practice recordings and related study data locally on your device. Your optional preparation profile (for example age band, education, and study preferences) is stored on this device and uses Android’s encrypted preferences when available.

The app does not require you to create an account.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app (subject to Android backup settings).

AI features and information sent to Google

When you generate questions, request answer guidance, current-affairs briefings, or similar AI features, the topic and relevant preparation details you chose may be sent to Google’s Gemini / Generative Language API so the app can create the requested content. Avoid entering information you do not want processed by that service.

Scanned or imported handwritten answer pages are kept in the app’s private storage on this device. On-device cropping and enhancement may be applied locally. AI review of handwritten pages is not available until Premium launches, and those pages are not sent for AI review at this time.

Interview practice may use the microphone to record answers on your device. Recordings are stored locally unless a future Premium feature you choose to use requires otherwise, which would be described in an updated policy.

Advertising and information handled by Google

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show advertisements, including rewarded ads (for example before AI generation or detailed news) and banner ads. Google’s SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Advertising or other device identifiers, where available;
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as launches, taps, and video views;
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as app launch time or errors.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to provide and measure ads, understand ad interactions, improve services, and help detect fraud. Data handled by Google’s SDK is transmitted using encryption in transit. Google’s handling of this information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s privacy information.

Retention and choices

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google through advertising or AI services is handled and retained under Google’s policies and settings; uninstalling the app does not delete information already processed by Google.

You can stop using online AI features at any time by not requesting generation or related AI actions. You can manage advertising and privacy settings through Android device settings and Google’s ad settings.

Children

Abhyas AI: BPSC Prep is intended for a general audience of exam aspirants and is not designed specifically for children under 13. The app does not ask users to submit personal information directly for account creation. Advertising and AI SDK data may still be handled as described in this policy. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the date at the top when we publish a revision.
